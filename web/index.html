What the Canadian rules say

A notice is not a court ruling

ISED explains that receiving a forwarded allegation does not itself establish infringement or mean you will be sued. A lawsuit is a separate process.

Review demands carefully

Section 41.25(3) prohibits settlement offers and requests for payment or personal information in a notice under this regime, including links to such demands. A phrase match alone cannot determine whether a notice complies.

Different documents need different action

ISED says the Notice and Notice regime does not require a subscriber to reply. That does not determine your response to court papers or other legal correspondence. For an actual claim or deadline, seek legal advice.

Your ISP forwards the allegation

Section 41.26 requires providers to forward compliant notices and retain identifying records. Subscriber information can be disclosed through a court process; this tool does not investigate who used a connection.
